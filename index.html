Privacy Policy — "Leh Qalaha?" app

Last updated: October 13, 2026

The app does not require an account, does not collect personal data, shows no ads, and uses no analytics or tracking. Saved hadiths, reading progress, recent searches and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device. When online, the app loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your device's IP address under Google's Privacy Policy. It also downloads translation files (hadith and story text in English) from a public GitHub Pages site, which may receive your device's IP address under GitHub's Privacy Statement; the files are cached on your device for offline use. Listening features use your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
